# Learning-design note

The study process is organised around a simple question: can I explain and apply a topic, rather than merely recognise its wording?

1. **Orient:** work through a manageable topic with source references.
2. **Recall:** use practice to reveal what has and has not been understood.
3. **Review:** return to mistakes and weaker areas over time.
4. **Reflect:** inspect progress and identify what to revisit or ask an instructor.

The distinction between coverage and understanding matters. Seeing every item once is different from explaining the reasoning or applying it in another context. Progress indicators therefore support review decisions rather than certify knowledge.

This page describes the intention behind the design. It does not publish teaching content, question examples or implementation rules, and it does not claim that the approach has been independently validated.
